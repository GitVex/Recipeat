# Database

Postgres runs as its own Compose project (`docker/compose.db.yaml`, container alias `recipeat-postgres`) on `recipeat-db-net`. It stays off the fetcher's network, and the app is the only client. The app queries through Kysely (`server/database/schema.ts`, `useKysely()`), and `jsonb` is written with its `json()` helper, never `JSON.stringify`. Migrations are plain `NNN_name.sql` files in `server/database/migrations/` (001 recipes, 002 collections, 003 tags, 004 preferences, 005 images, 006 ingredients, 007 ingredient matching). They run at startup under an advisory lock, one transaction per file, recorded in `schema_migrations`. A failed migration stops the app from serving, and an applied file is never edited. Without `NUXT_DATABASE_URL`, storage routes answer 503.

The tables: recipes are owned by `owner_sub`, with lineage columns and one pinned version per line. Collections hold versions in order. Tags hang on a line's root and are unique per owner regardless of case. Preferences are one `jsonb` document per person, checked against `PREFERENCES` in `shared/utils/preferences.ts`. Images are `BYTEA` rows in two browser-made sizes, read only through `/api/images/{id}`. Every foreign key between a person's rows carries `owner_sub`. Backups are manual: `docker exec recipeat-postgres pg_dump -U recipeat recipeat`.

The ingredient store (#170) is `ingredients`, its `ingredient_names` per language (unique by `lower(name)`, with a `pg_trgm` index in `public` for close matches) and `ingredient_sources`. An empty density means nobody has measured it. It is seeded once by `scripts/seed-ingredients.ts` from the Open Food Facts ingredients that carry a USDA FoodData Central id, with densities from FDC's cup and spoon portions. Ingredient names from [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org), available under the [Open Database License (ODbL)](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/); densities from [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov) (SR Legacy, public domain). After a save, a trigger queues the recipe in `ingredient_queue` and notifies; workers in `server/plugins/ingredients.ts` (`NUXT_INGREDIENT_WORKERS`, default 2, plus a scan every `NUXT_INGREDIENT_SCAN_MINUTES`) claim recipes with `SKIP LOCKED` and match each line, writing `ingredient_links` and, for cooks who opted in, `ingredient_candidates`. Both keep the line's name, and a row whose line no longer carries it is stale.
