# Planning

Built: Zitadel login; import from text, photo, website and Instagram posts; Postgres storage with images; recipe lineage (save overwrites, a progression is a new version that takes the line's pin, a variant starts a line of its own, and deleting a version takes its progressions with it); collections, tags, filters, preferences, scaling, US/metric display, printing and similar-recipe search. Work is scheduled in Waves by the rules in [repo_rules.md](repo_rules.md); open work lives in the GitHub issues and milestones.

Current direction: a central ingredient store (#170). It is seeded from Open Food Facts entries that have an FDC id, and after a recipe is saved each ingredient line is fuzzy matched against it; cooks who opt in confirm close matches, which adds aliases and new entries. Shopping lists (#84), the pantry, translation and substitutions build on its ids. Known gaps: a photo of two pages comes back as one blended recipe; the app can't tell how well the model read a page; and every text and photo import sends the user's recipe to Google.
