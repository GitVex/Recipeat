# Design

Recipeat looks like a kitchen notebook: warm paper surfaces, olive ink, one terracotta accent, Playfair Display for names (the key word in italic orange) and DM Sans for everything else. Every colour, size, radius, shadow and motion value is a custom property on `:root` in `app/assets/main.css`, and the stylesheet uses nothing else. Text that is read meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) on `--wash`. `--faint` is only for things that aren't read, and 7px (`--text-tag`) is only for tags and badges. Dark mode is out of scope for now.

The app reads left to right, broad to particular: opening a recipe from `/recipes` folds the list into a rail, and going back reverses it. Movement on the same screen crossfades, moving to another place fades out and in, and overlays rise and sink. Only opacity and transform animate, apart from the collection's columns, and pages never take a transform. Leaving is quicker than arriving (`--dur-exit`/`--dur-enter`/`--dur-move`). All of it is plain Vue `<Transition>`, and reduced motion turns it off.
